Flagship · 2026 · Python

AI-Powered NSE Stock Market Analyzer

Research platform · paper replay, no live trading

A quantitative research platform for equities on India's National Stock Exchange. It combines technical analysis, machine learning with look-ahead-free features, news sentiment, and RAG over classic trading literature. A LangGraph multi-agent pipeline debates each trade idea, and deterministic risk math can veto anything the LLMs propose.
